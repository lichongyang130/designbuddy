# Photography for the four independent samples

These are real photographs/scientific photographic images, not AI-generated placeholders.
Per-image credit, original URL and licensing evidence are listed in sources.json.
scikit-image's v0.25.2 _fetchers.py documents the licenses for these specific datasets.
NASA/Hubble and SpaceX images are identified there as public domain; camera, cat,
coffee, brick, grass, gravel, cell and retina images are CC0. No endorsement is implied.
The two exif-samples photographs are supplied under that repository's CC BY-SA 4.0
contribution terms. Their WebP derivatives remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Images have been resized, re-encoded and stripped of metadata. Originals are linked.
The travel collection and the brand's leaf/lamp photographs use the separate existing
inspiration/sources.json attribution. Collections do not overlap between the four samples.
Microscopy and retina images illustrate a report layout, not diagnosis or new research.
