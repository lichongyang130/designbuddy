# FORME concept photography

Five new AI-generated concept images created specifically for this fictional furniture procurement prototype: room.webp (editorial living room), chair.webp (walnut lounge chair), sofa.webp (bouclé sofa), table.webp (travertine table), lamp.webp (paper floor lamp).

These are not photographs of purchasable products, a real manufacturer or a customer's home. Product specifications, prices and delivery policies are invented demonstration data. Variant controls update configuration and pricing, not the generated photograph; images do not prove structural performance, sustainability or exact material/color reproduction. No third-party product endorsement is implied. Exports carry this disclosure. Source images are resized/compressed to local WebP; full-resolution generated intermediates are excluded from Git.
