# Video footage provenance

The footage in these fictional preview concepts is reused across cases. The 360p H.264 files were obtained from the [Mixpeek video embedding benchmark](https://github.com/mixpeek/video-embedding-benchmark/tree/main/data/videos). That repository's download script maps filenames to the Pexels item IDs below. The benchmark has misleading filenames and descriptions for several clips; the scene captions here were written from frames inspected from the actual files. Posters were extracted locally at one second from each video.

The benchmark describes the material as CC0, but Pexels has its own [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). We do **not** represent the clips as CC0; review that license and any applicable rights in people, brands, and places before production use. The IDs below are the upstream mapping, not independently verified artist attribution. No video should imply that people or brands shown endorse these fictional concepts. The materials are presented as embedded case previews, not a downloadable stock footage catalog.
